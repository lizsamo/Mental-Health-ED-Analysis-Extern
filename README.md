# Mental-Health-ED-Analysis-Extern
Mental Health-Related Emergency Department Visit Trends

## Project Overview

This project analyzes mental health-related emergency department visit
rates using data from the CDC National Syndromic Surveillance Program.

The primary research question is:

**Which mental health conditions contributed most to growth in U.S.
emergency department demand from 2022 through 2026?**

## Business and Public Health Relevance

Understanding changes in mental health-related emergency department
utilization can help healthcare organizations identify growing areas of
demand, improve staffing decisions, and support resource planning.

## Dataset

Source: CDC National Syndromic Surveillance Program

The dataset includes:

- Month
- Mental health condition
- Demographic category
- Demographic value
- Emergency department visit rate
- Geographic information

## Data Cleaning

The cleaning process included:

- Converting month values into datetime format
- Checking for missing values
- Checking for duplicate records
- Standardizing text categories
- Separating demographic groups
- Validating numeric columns
- Sorting observations chronologically

## Analysis

The analysis focuses on:

- Mental health emergency department trends over time
- Growth rates by mental health condition
- Comparisons across demographic groups
- Conditions contributing most to overall demand growth

download is required.
